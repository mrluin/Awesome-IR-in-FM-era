# 🤝 Contributing

Help keep this list useful, accurate, and easy to browse.

- **Choose one primary category.** Follow the Survey taxonomy, including Restoration-Oriented Foundation Models, and use a cross-reference when a paper is relevant elsewhere.
- **Keep the scope precise.** Distinguish restoration methods and quality signals from foundational background, conventional baselines, task-specific generative context, datasets, evaluation resources, and related surveys.
- **Use the formal title.** Retain the paper's established method acronym and terminology. Add only a short, specific description of its contribution.
- **Check publication metadata.** Give the verified venue and year. For preprints, name the actual hosting platform, such as `arXiv 2026` or `Preprints.org 2026`; use the capitalization `arXiv`. Do not infer conference acceptance from an arXiv submission.
- **Link to primary sources.** Use the publisher, official proceedings, arXiv record, or an author-controlled paper page. Search-result links are not paper links.
- **Verify optional resources.** Include Code or Project only when the paper or an official author resource identifies it. Omit unconfirmed links.
- **Keep the reading order consistent.** Within each smallest paper category, sort by the displayed publication year from newest to oldest. For papers from the same year, sort alphabetically by the formal paper title (case-insensitive). Do not infer the year from an arXiv identifier.
- **Avoid duplicate entries.** A preprint and its published version belong to the same entry. Prefer the official publication link when available.
- **Make corrections traceable.** Include the source supporting a new link or metadata change. Flag disagreements with the Survey bibliography for review.
- **Check the Markdown.** Confirm local links and heading anchors work, and keep entries readable on a narrow screen.

Use this compact entry style:

```markdown
- **Formal paper title** (Method, only if not already in the title)\
  *Venue Year* · [📄 Paper](official-paper-url) · [💻 Code](verified-official-code-url) · [🌐 Project](verified-official-project-url)
```

Keep the complete formal title in bold and put an optional method name in plain parentheses. Do not repeat a method name already present in the title. Keep venue/year italicized on the next line; indent that line by two spaces and end the title line with a backslash for a Markdown line break. Leave one blank line between entries.

For a preprint, use its actual platform and recorded year in the venue/year position: `arXiv 2026` for an arXiv Paper link, or `Preprints.org 2026` for a Preprints.org Paper link. Keep the recorded year unchanged. Retain a verified conference or journal label even when its Paper link points to arXiv. Omit an unverified Code or Project link rather than adding a placeholder. Do not add popularity counts, guessed links, or unsupported publication claims.
